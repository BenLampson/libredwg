Internet Archive AutoCAD 2022 media audit
==========================================

Audit date: 2026-07-11

Purpose
-------

Determine whether original AutoCAD 2022 product media emits the experimental
AC103-4/R_2022b identifier, classify all included DWG and DWT files, and verify
the current-format files through strict C blocking-versus-Stream parity.

Classification
--------------

The extracted product tree contains 232 unique CAD files: 160 DWGs and 72
DWTs. Their complete header and internal-version distribution is:

  AC1021/0x1b: 144
  AC1021/0x1f: 1
  AC1024/0x1d: 60
  AC1032/0x21: 27

The old-format files are samples and templates intentionally shipped with the
product. None of the 232 files uses AC103-4. The 27 current-format files all use
the normal AC1032 header and internal version 0x21.

This original-product evidence agrees with Autodesk's public DWG version enum,
which identifies the 2018-through-2022 family as AC1032 and does not define
AC103-4. LibreDWG retains AC103-4/R_2022b as a synthetic dispatch guard, but it
is not treated as a missing historical product format.

Strict parity result
--------------------

  LIBREDWG_STREAM_TEST_REFS=1

  Stream decode errors: 0
  full: 0 for every file
